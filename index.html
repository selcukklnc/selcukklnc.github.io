AI in Education · Learning Sciences · STEM Education

Designing research-informed AI learning environments for inquiry, reflection, and meaningful participation.

I am a Postdoctoral Research Scholar at the University at Albany, SUNY, specializing in AI-supported teaching and learning in science and STEM education. My research focuses on AI literacy, generative AI, and mixed-methods studies of how AI tools support inquiry, reflection, assessment, and pedagogical decision-making across K–12 and higher education contexts.

Profile

Academic focus

My work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence in education, learning sciences, STEM education, and educational technology. I design and study learning environments where AI systems, chatbot mentors, and IoT-enabled tools support students' inquiry processes, data practices, computational thinking, and engagement with authentic problems.

With a PhD in Science Education and an MS in Software Engineering, I approach AI in education as both a design and research problem. My scholarship connects technical systems, pedagogical design, classroom implementation, mixed-methods analysis, and responsible adoption of emerging technologies.

Research

Research areas

Generative AI in Education

Student and educator perceptions, use, and critical engagement with generative AI in teaching, learning, assessment, and academic work.

AI Literacy and AI-TPACK

Teacher and learner competencies for understanding, evaluating, and integrating AI in pedagogically meaningful and ethical ways.

AI-Supported STEM Learning

Chatbot-supported inquiry, intelligent tutoring, engineering design, computational thinking, and data practices in STEM contexts.

IoT and Citizen Science

Real-time environmental sensing, citizen science, and authentic inquiry through Arduino-based sensor systems and classroom data work.
