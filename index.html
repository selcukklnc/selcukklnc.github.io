About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University at Albany, State University of New York, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, educational technology, and learning sciences. Drawing on a background in software engineering (MS) and science education (PhD), I design and study AI-driven learning systems, IoT-enabled sensor networks, and adaptive tutoring environments that support meaningful and equitable learning.

My scholarship appears in high-impact venues including Computers & Education, Teaching and Teacher Education, and proceedings from AERA, ISLS, and ACM conferences. I use mixed-methods and design-based research to examine issues such as ethical AI assessment, AI-TPACK development, computational thinking, and technology integration in diverse K–12 and higher education contexts.

Much of my recent work focuses on AI literacy, generative AI in education, and rural agricultural STEM (Ag-STEM) education, where I co-design AI-enhanced curricula with teachers, develop AI chatbot mentors and Arduino-based IoT systems, and investigate how these tools shape students' inquiry, reasoning, and data literacy. I also contribute to competitive grant proposals, mentor teachers and graduate students, and serve as a reviewer and organizer for international journals and conferences. Across projects, my goal is to translate cutting-edge AI and educational technology research into practical, responsible, and scalable solutions for classrooms, teacher preparation, and online learning environments.
