AI · Rural Education · Ag-STEM

Artificial Intelligence Mentorship for Rural Education

This project focused on designing and implementing an AI-enhanced agriculture-STEM learning environment for middle school students in rural school settings.

Purpose

To support students’ inquiry, computational thinking, and data practices through AI mentorship and authentic agricultural problems.

Design

Integrated custom AI chatbot tutors with Arduino-based IoT sensor systems in a 16-week curriculum across rural school districts.

Educational impact

Supported student engagement with real-world data, teacher professional development, and research outputs for publications and conferences.

Chatbot · Engineering Design · Permaculture

Permaculture Chatbot & Engineering-Design Mentorship

This project explored how AI-supported mentorship can scaffold ecological design thinking, iterative problem-solving, and learner engagement.

Purpose

To support students’ permaculture learning and engineering-design thinking through an AI-supported mentorship model.

Design

Developed a chatbot within a design-based research framework to help students refine permaculture design projects.

Educational impact

Generated design insights into how AI mentorship can support ecological reasoning and student-centered design work.

IoT · Citizen Science · Air Quality

IoT-Enabled Citizen Science: Real-Time Air Quality Monitoring

This dissertation-based project created an authentic citizen-science learning experience for preservice science teachers through real-time environmental data collection and analysis.

Purpose

To connect science teacher education with citizen science, environmental inquiry, and real-world data interpretation.

Design

Designed and built a WHO-aligned Arduino-based mobile air-quality monitoring system measuring particulate matter and gas pollutants.

Educational impact

Supported preservice teachers’ inquiry practices, data interpretation, and engagement with environmental science issues.

STEM Education · Higher Education · Policy

Determining of Education Faculties’ STEM Readiness

This scientific research project examined STEM readiness among faculty members and preservice teachers following the integration of STEM education into the curriculum.

Purpose

To investigate institutional readiness, faculty perceptions, and preservice teachers’ preparation for STEM education.

Design

Used survey and interview data to examine instructional technology use, interdisciplinary collaboration, and perceptions of STEM.

Educational impact

Contributed to policy-oriented understanding of STEM readiness and professional development needs in higher education.
