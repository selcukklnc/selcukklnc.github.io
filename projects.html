AI · Rural Education · Ag-STEM

Artificial Intelligence Mentorship for Rural Education

Educational Innovation Project · January 2025 - January 2026 · Texas, USA

This project focused on designing and implementing a 16-week AI-enhanced agriculture-STEM curriculum for 80+ middle school students across rural school settings.

Purpose

To support students' computational thinking, inquiry, and data-driven environmental investigation through AI mentorship and authentic agricultural problems.

Design

Integrated custom AI chatbot tutors, Arduino-based IoT sensors, real-time data practices, and teacher-supported classroom implementation.

Educational impact

Supported implementation across Caldwell and Brenham Junior High Schools while generating data for conference presentations and journal manuscripts.

Chatbot · Engineering Design · Permaculture

Permaculture Chatbot & Engineering-Design Mentorship

Educational Design Project · October 2024 - September 2025 · Turkey

This project explored how AI-supported mentorship can scaffold ecological design thinking, iterative problem-solving, and learner engagement in permaculture design.

Purpose

To support students' permaculture learning and engineering-design thinking through an AI-supported mentorship model.

Design

Implemented an intervention with approximately 70 students, including a two-day introductory unit and a two-month project phase using a custom chatbot.

Educational impact

Iteratively refined the system using feedback, interaction data, and learner needs to generate design insights for ecological reasoning and student-centered design work.

IoT · Citizen Science · Air Quality

IoT-Enabled Citizen Science: Real-Time Air Quality Monitoring

Dissertation Project · September 2022 - February 2023 · Turkey

This dissertation-based project created an authentic citizen-science learning experience for preservice science teachers through real-time environmental data collection and analysis.

Purpose

To connect science teacher education with citizen science, environmental inquiry, research question development, and real-world data interpretation.

Design

Designed and built a WHO-aligned Arduino-based mobile air-quality monitoring system measuring PM2.5, PM10, and key gas pollutants with real-time display and data logging.

Educational impact

Supported 12 preservice teachers in collecting, analyzing, visualizing, and presenting air-quality data across diverse campus locations, developing recommendations for university leadership, and documenting the project in a short field video.

STEM Education · Higher Education · Policy

Determining of Education Faculties’ STEM Readiness

Scientific Research Project · January 2017 - December 2017 · Turkey

This scientific research project examined STEM readiness among faculty members and preservice teachers following the integration of STEM education into the curriculum.

Purpose

To investigate institutional readiness, faculty perceptions, and preservice teachers’ preparation for STEM education.

Design

Used survey and interview data to examine instructional technology use, interdisciplinary collaboration, and perceptions of STEM.

Educational impact

Produced a policy report that informed Turkish Higher Education Council professional-development and curriculum-reform efforts.
