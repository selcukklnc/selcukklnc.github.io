Research

Research agenda

My research examines how AI-supported systems can shape inquiry, reflection, assessment, data practices, teacher learning, pedagogical decision-making, and responsible engagement with emerging technologies.

Research overview

My work brings together artificial intelligence in education, learning sciences, science and STEM education, educational technology, and software engineering. I am particularly interested in how AI can support learners and educators in complex learning environments, not only by automating tasks, but by scaffolding reasoning, inquiry, reflection, design, assessment, and responsible decision-making.

Across my studies, I combine design-based research with mixed-methods analysis to examine how emerging technologies function in real classrooms, teacher education settings, higher education contexts, interdisciplinary STEM learning environments, and AI-mediated environments where trust, privacy, and misinformation matter.

Generative AI in Education

I study how students and educators perceive, use, and critically evaluate generative AI tools in educational contexts. This includes learning, assessment, academic integrity, productivity, student-AI collaboration, and responsible adoption.

AI Literacy and AI-TPACK

I examine how teachers and learners develop the knowledge needed to understand, evaluate, and integrate AI into teaching and learning. This includes AI literacy, AI-TPACK, teacher preparation, professional development, and ethical decision-making.

AI-Supported STEM Learning Environments

I design and investigate AI-supported learning environments where chatbot tutors, real-time data dashboards, and intelligent systems scaffold students' inquiry, data practices, computational thinking, engineering design, and problem-solving.

IoT, Citizen Science, and Real-Time Data

My work also explores how IoT-enabled sensor systems can help learners engage with authentic environmental data. These projects connect classroom learning with citizen science, place-based inquiry, and real-world STEM problems.

Assessment, Ethics, and Policy in AI in Education

I am interested in how educational institutions develop responsible assessment practices, ethical guidelines, and policy responses around AI. This includes academic integrity, AI-assisted assessment, and governance of generative AI.

AI Trust, Data Exposure, and Educational GenAI Risk

My current work also examines trust boundaries in AI-mediated environments, including prompt leakage, sensitive data exposure, misinformation, and behavioral cybersecurity concerns that emerge when generative AI becomes part of educational practice.

Methodological approach

  • Mixed-methods research in AI-supported educational contexts
  • Design-based research for developing and refining learning environments
  • Qualitative analysis of interviews, observations, student artifacts, classroom discourse, and teacher reflections
  • Survey design, validation, and quantitative analysis
  • Learning analytics, log data, multimodal classroom data, and interaction analysis
  • Research-practice partnerships with teachers, schools, and interdisciplinary teams
  • Analysis of trust, privacy, data exposure, and misinformation risks in educational GenAI use
