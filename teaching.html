Teaching & Mentoring

Preparing teachers, mentoring researchers, and designing technology-enhanced learning experiences.

My teaching and mentoring work spans teacher education, science methods courses, school-based practicums, K–12 STEM instruction, teacher professional development, and AI/educational technology integration.

Teaching approach

Teaching as design, inquiry, and reflective practice

My teaching approach is grounded in inquiry-based science education, learning sciences, and educational technology. I view teaching as a design-oriented practice in which learners engage with authentic problems, build explanations, use evidence, and reflect on their learning processes.

Across higher education and K–12 contexts, I have supported preservice teachers, graduate students, classroom teachers, and students in designing technology-enhanced learning experiences. My work emphasizes inquiry, assessment, AI literacy, computational thinking, data practices, and responsible use of emerging technologies.

Graduate-level teaching and research mentoring

I have supported graduate students in educational research, instructional technology, qualitative and quantitative methods, mixed-methods research design, and technology-enhanced science education projects.

  • Mentored graduate students in designing and refining research questions, conceptual frameworks, data collection strategies, and analysis plans.
  • Supported students in developing technology-enhanced science education projects, including AI-supported instructional tools and sensor-based inquiry activities.
  • Guided students in qualitative, quantitative, and mixed-methods approaches, including interviews, classroom observations, artifact analysis, survey design, and interpretation of educational data.
  • Provided feedback on academic writing, research presentations, project reports, and emerging scholarly work.

AI, digital learning, and educational technology integration

A major part of my teaching and mentoring work involves helping educators and students use emerging technologies in pedagogically meaningful ways. Rather than treating technology as an add-on, I focus on how AI and digital tools can support inquiry, feedback, assessment, reflection, and student agency.

  • Designed and facilitated learning experiences involving generative AI, custom GPTs, AI-supported instructional tools, and digital learning platforms.
  • Supported teachers and students in using AI tools ethically, critically, and productively in STEM learning contexts.
  • Connected AI literacy with broader instructional design goals, including assessment, classroom implementation, and responsible pedagogical decision-making.
  • Helped design technology-enhanced learning activities involving IoT sensors, real-time environmental data, Arduino systems, and student-led inquiry.
