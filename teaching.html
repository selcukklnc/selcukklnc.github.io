Teaching & Mentoring

Supporting learners, teachers, and emerging researchers.

My teaching and mentoring work spans higher education, K–12 STEM contexts, teacher professional development, research methods, and technology-enhanced learning design.

University-level instruction and mentoring

At Middle East Technical University, I supported and taught undergraduate and graduate courses in instructional technology, research methods, science pedagogy, assessment, and laboratory applications in science education.

  • Designed and supported project-based learning activities aligned with inquiry-based STEM education.
  • Integrated IoT-based environmental monitoring projects using Arduino systems.
  • Supported students in designing AI-supported instructional tools and sensor-based inquiry activities.
  • Mentored graduate students in qualitative, quantitative, and mixed-methods research design.

Teacher professional development

I have worked with K–12 teachers and schools on professional learning related to inquiry-based teaching, STEM integration, AI literacy, instructional design, digital tools, and student-centered assessment.

  • Facilitated workshops on inquiry-based and interdisciplinary curriculum design.
  • Coached teachers through classroom observations, feedback conferences, and collaborative planning.
  • Designed and delivered sessions on ethical and productive use of generative AI.
  • Supported teachers in integrating AI tools and sensor data into classroom instruction.

K–12 classroom and practicum experience

My teaching background also includes direct K–12 classroom instruction and supervised teaching practicums in high school contexts.

  • Designed and delivered curriculum-aligned chemistry lessons for high school students.
  • Integrated hands-on laboratory experiments and digital resources.
  • Collaborated with mentor teachers on lesson planning, laboratory management, and student support.
